01 · Why Malaysia

Why study in Malaysia

Malaysia gives you world-ranked degrees, branch campuses of British and Australian universities, food from three great cuisines and festivals every month, about four hours from home.

01391

EMGS institutions

Research universities, private universities, branch campuses and colleges, all registered for international students.

02RM 14k

Public tuition, per year, from

Typical annual tuition at a public university. Private universities cost more but stay well below UK, US or Australian fees.

0312months

Graduate Pass

Stay on after a bachelor's degree or higher, with part-time work in approved sectors while you plan your next move.

04UK·AU

Branch campuses

Including Monash, Nottingham, Southampton, Heriot-Watt, Curtin and Swinburne, awarding the same degree certificate as their home campuses.

053cultures

One campus

Malay, Chinese and Indian festivals fill the calendar, Tamil is widely spoken and South Indian food is everywhere.

06~4hours

From Chennai

A short-notice trip home is easy, and lectures and exams all run in English.

        07 · Life in Malaysia

        Jom makan. Then celebrate.

        Malay, Chinese and Indian Malaysia share the same streets, so your weekly food bill stays small and the festival calendar stays full.

        09 · Scholarships, loans & work

        Fund it smart, work the breaks.

        Three ways to pay for your degree, all arranged before you fly. Hover a card to see how we help.

        01RM
        Merit awards, bursaries and grants

        Scholarships

        We match strong profiles to institutional scholarships that can cover part or all of tuition, and coach you through the essays that win them.

        • Merit and need-based awards
        • Essay and interview coaching
        • Deadlines tracked for you
        02RM
        With or without collateral

        Education loans

        Direct tie-ups with national banks and private lenders cover tuition and accommodation, with the paperwork handled alongside your offer letter.

        • Collateral and non-collateral
        • Tuition plus living costs
        • 10 lending partners
        03RM
        Earning in Malaysia, the real rules

        Vacation work

        No work during term. In semester breaks longer than seven days you may work up to 20 hours a week in restaurants, petrol kiosks, mini markets or hotels, with prior Immigration Department approval.

        • Up to 20 hours a week in breaks
        • Approved sectors only
        • Your institution applies for permission
